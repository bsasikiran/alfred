# Demo Example — Football Time Change

## Trigger

The son's football training moves from **17:00 to 16:30**.

## Household context

- The son must leave home at **16:05**.
- Dad is scheduled to work at the office.
- Mom must collect the daughter at **16:15**.
- Both journeys require an adult.

## Conflict

No parent can complete both journeys on time under the original plan.

## Options

1. **Dad leaves work early** and takes the son to football.
2. **Mom takes the son first**, while the daughter uses a pre-approved alternative pickup.
3. **The son misses training**, avoiding transport changes but sacrificing the activity.

## Recommendation

Recommend Option 1, provided Dad is allowed to leave early. It protects both children's commitments and introduces the smallest household disruption.

## Approval prompt

> Dad can leave work at 15:35 and take the son to football. This keeps both children's commitments. Should I apply this to the proposed family plan?

## Final briefing

> Football has moved to 16:30. Dad should leave work at 15:35 and leave home with the son at 16:05. Mom collects the daughter at 16:15. No other commitments are affected.
